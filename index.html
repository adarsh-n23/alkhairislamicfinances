Frequently Asked Questions About Halal Loans in India

Quick, authentic answers about our Sharia principles, approval criteria, and ethical loan options.

What is Al Khair Islamic Finance?

Al Khair Islamic Finance is India's leading Sharia-compliant financial institution providing 100% halal, interest-free (zero Riba) personal loans up to ₹10 Lakhs, business loans up to ₹50 Lakhs, home loans up to ₹1 Crore, education loans, and vehicle loans with rapid 24-48 hour approval across all 28 Indian states.

Are Al Khair loans genuinely 100% interest-free (zero Riba)?

Yes, completely. Islamic law strictly prohibits Riba (usury/interest). Al Khair does not charge any predetermined interest or compounding interest fees. Instead, financing operates under recognized Sharia contracts such as Murabaha (cost-plus sale markup) and Diminishing Musharaka (equity partnership) with absolute transparency.

Can Non-Muslims also apply for an interest-free Islamic loan?

Yes! Islamic banking is an ethical economic model open to every Indian citizen aged 18 and above, regardless of faith or community. Anyone seeking transparent, non-predatory, interest-free capital is welcome to apply.

What documents are required to apply online?

Applying online requires only minimal documentation: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, applicant passport photograph, recent bank statement or salary slip, and valid address proof. The submission process is 100% paperless.

How long does the sanction and disbursal process take?

Eligible applications are reviewed by our verification team within 24 to 48 hours. Upon approval, an official sanction letter is generated, and disbursal is initiated directly to your active bank account.
